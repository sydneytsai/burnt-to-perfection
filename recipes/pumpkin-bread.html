Pumpkin Bread

Moist, rich, and perfectly spiced.

Dessert ⏱ ~65 min 🌿 Vegetarian Easy
← All Recipes
dessert

🧺 What You'll Need

  • Unsalted butter180 g
  • Pumpkin pie spice4 tsp
  • Brown sugar70 g
  • White sugar70 g
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Vanilla1 tsp
  • Pumpkin1 can
  • Eggs2
  • All purpose flour320 g
  • Baking soda1 tsp
  • Baking powder1 tsp

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).

  2. 2

    Brown the butter and let it cool.

  3. 3

    Combine all the wet ingredients in a large bowl.

  4. 4

    Combine all the dry ingredients in a bowl.

  5. 5

    Pour the dry ingredients into the wet, and combine thoroughly.

  6. 6

    Pour the batter into a parchment lined (or butter greased) loaf pan.

  7. 7

    Bake for 50 minutes, turning the pan 180 degrees halfway through.

  8. 8

    Let it cool for 15 minutes and enjoy!

  9. 9

    Pumpkin bread loaf can be left out at room temperature for around 2-4 days.

  10. 10

    Store in the fridge for up to 7 days.

  11. 11

    Slice and store in the freezer for up to 3 months.

Ingredients

  • unsalted butter (180 g)
  • pumpkin pie spice (4 tsp)
  • brown sugar (70 g)

  • baking powder (1 tsp)
