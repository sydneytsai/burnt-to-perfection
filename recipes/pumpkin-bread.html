Pumpkin Bread

Moist, rich, and perfectly spiced.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 180g unsalted butter
  • 4 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 70g brown sugar
  • 70g white sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 can pumpkin
  • 2 eggs
  • 320g All Purpose Flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Brown the butter and let it cool.
  3. Combine all the wet ingredients in a large bowl.
  4. Combine all the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  5. Pour the dry ingredients into the wet, and combine thoroughly.
  6. Pour the batter into a parchment lined (or butter greased) loaf pan.
  7. Bake for 50 minutes, turning the pan 180 degrees halfway through.
  8. Let it cool for 15 minutes and enjoy!

Storage instructions

  • Pumpkin bread loaf can be left out at room temperature for around 2-4 days.
  • Store in the fridge for up to 7 days.
  • Slice and store in the freezer for up to 3 months.
