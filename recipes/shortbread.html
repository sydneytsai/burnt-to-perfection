Short Bread

Buttery, fun, and crisp.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • salted butter (12 tbsp, room temp)
  • sugar (1/2 cup)
  • vanilla (1/2 tsp)
  • flour (8.75 oz)

Instructions

  1. Cream together the butter and sugar.
  2. Add vanilla and stir.
  3. Mix in flour.
  4. Shape into 1/2 inch thick cookies.
  5. Chill for 1 hour.
  6. Preheat the oven to 350 F.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes.
