Cardamom Buns

Spicy, sweet, and pillowy.

Dessert ⏱ ~3 hrs Medium 🌿 Vegetarian
← All Recipes
dessert

🌾 Dough

  • Milk300 g
  • Active dry yeast5 g
  • All purpose flour500 g
  • Salt6 g
  • Cane sugar85 g
  • Cardamom1 tbsp
  • Unsalted butter85 g, softened

🍯 Filling

  • Unsalted butter120 g, softened
  • Light brown sugar1/2 cup
  • Cane sugar1/4 cup
  • All purpose flour1 tbsp
  • Salt1 pinch
  • Nutmegpinch
  • Orange zestfrom 1/2 orange
  • Cardamom1 tbsp

✨ Topping

  • Cardamom1 tsp
  • Cane sugar1 tbsp
  • Egg yolks2
  • Milksplash

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Whisk together flour, sugar, salt, and cardamon.

  2. 2

    In a larger separate bowl, whisk together milk (make sure the milk is at room temperature) and yeast.

  3. 3

    Dump the dry ingedients into the wet ingredients bowl and combine until a dough forms.

  4. 4

    Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 20 minutes until smooth.

  5. 5

    Place dough in a lightly oil bowl and cover. Place in the fridge for 12-24 hours.

  6. 6

    Next day, combine filling ingredients.

  7. 7

    Remove the dough from the fridge. Lightly flour a work surface and roll out the dough to a rough 12x18 rectangle.

  8. 8

    Spread butter mixture with a spatula evenly across the entire surface.

  9. 9

    From the shorter edge, fold the dough down 1/3 of the way. Then fold the other 1/3 up like a letter.

  10. 10

    Wrap the dough and let it rest in the fridge for 15-20 minutes.

  11. 11

    Roll out the dough slightly to 15x12.

  12. 12

    Cut the dough into 12 even strips.

  13. 13

    Working on one strip at a time, elongate the strip to around 24 inches, wrap around your hand a couple times then wrap the end of the strip around like a ring binding the strands together.

  14. 14

    Place buns on a lined baking sheet and let it proof for 1 - 1.5 hours.

  15. 15

    Once proofed, Preheat oven to 425.

  16. 16

    Combine the cardamom and sugar in a bowl.

  17. 17

    Combine the milk and egg yolks in a separate bowl.

  18. 18

    Brush milk and egg mixture over the buns and sprinkle some of the sugar mixture on each one.

  19. 19

    Bake for 7 minutes. Lower temperature to 350F, rotate pans and bake for an additional 6-7 minutes or until browned and baked to an internal temp of 190.

  20. 20

    Let the buns cool for 20 minutes then enjoy!

Ingredients

  • unsalted butter (85 g, softened)

  • light brown sugar (1/2 cup)
  • cane sugar (1/4 cup)
  • all purpose flour (1 tbsp)
  • salt (1 pinch)
  • nutmeg (pinch)
  • orange zest (from 1/2 orange)
  • cardamom (1 tsp)
  • cane sugar (1 tbsp)
  • egg yolks (2)
  • milk (splash)
