Cardamom Buns

Spicy sweet cardamom buns.

← Back to search

Dough Ingredients

  • 300g milk (room temp)
  • 5g active dry yeast
  • 500g All Purpose Flour
  • 6g salt
  • 85g cane sugar
  • 1 tbsp cardamom
  • 85g unsalted butter (softened)

Filling Ingredients

  • 120g unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1/2 cup light brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup cane sugar
  • 1 tbsp All Purpose Flour
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Zest of 1/2 an orange
  • 1 tbsp cardamom

Topping Ingredients

  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • 1 tbsp cane sugar
  • 2 egg yolks
  • Splash of milk

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, sugar, salt, and cardamon.
  2. In a larger separate bowl, whisk together milk and yeast.
  3. Dump the dry ingedients into the wet ingredients bowl and combine until a dough forms.
  4. Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 20 minutes until smooth.
  5. Place dough in a lightly oil bowl and cover. Place in the fridge for 12-24 hours.
  6. Next day, combine filling ingredients.
  7. Remove the dough from the fridge. Lightly flour a work surface and roll out the dough to a rough 12x18 rectangle.
  8. Spread butter mixture with a spatula evenly across the entire surface.
  9. From the shorter edge, fold the dough down 1/3 of the way. Then fold the other 1/3 up like a letter.
  10. Wrap the dough and let it rest in the fridge for 15-20 minutes.
  11. Roll out the dough slightly to 15x12.
  12. Cut the dough into 12 even strips.
  13. Working on one strip at a time, elongate the strip to around 24 inches, wrap around your hand a couple times then wrap the end of the strip around like a ring binding the strands together.
  14. Place buns on a lined baking sheet and let it proof for 1 - 1.5 hours.
  15. Once proofed, Preheat oven to 425.
  16. Combine the cardamom and sugar in a bowl.
  17. Combine the milk and egg yolks in a separate bowl.
  18. Brush milk and egg mixture over the buns and sprinkle some of the sugar mixture on each one.
  19. Bake for 7 minutes. Lower temperature to 350F, rotate pans and bake for an additional 6-7 minutes or until browned and baked to an internal temp of 190.
  20. Let the buns cool for 20 minutes then enjoy!
