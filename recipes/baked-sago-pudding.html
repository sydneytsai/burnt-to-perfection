Baked Sago Pudding

Creamy and rich Hong Kong dessert.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 70g sago
  • 30g custard powder
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 70g sugar
  • 80 ml evaporated milk
  • 20g butter

Instructions

  1. Cook sago on medium heat for 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Wash sago under cold water once cooked and set aside.
  3. Add the other 1/2 of the milk, evaporated milk, butter, and sugar in a pot.
  4. Cook the milk mixture under medium heat at a simmer (do not boil).
  5. While that is cooking, in a bowl, mix together custard powder, 1/2 of the milk, and eggs.
  6. Gradually add the custard mixture into the simmering milk mixture and stir constantly.
  7. The mixture should start to thicken significantly.
  8. Once thickened, add the sago.
  9. Pour the mixture into ramekins and placed onto a large sheet tray.
  10. Create a water bath in the sheet tray and bake the puddings for 20-25 minutes.
  11. Pudding is ready when there are light brown spots on the surface.
  12. Let it cool for 10 minutes and enjoy!
