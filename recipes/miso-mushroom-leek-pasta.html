Miso Mushroom Leek Pasta

Creamy umami pasta.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 medium leeks
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1.5 lbs mushrooms
  • 1 lb pasta
  • 3/4 cup parmesan
  • 1 tbsp sherry or red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta until al dente.
  2. Heat up the olive oil in a pan.
  3. Saute leeks for 5 minutes.
  4. Brown the mushroom (around 10 minutes).
  5. Add the sherry or vinegar and cook off the alcohol.
  6. Stir in the miso paste.
  7. Add the pasta and some pasta water with the parmesan and stir until a thick creamy sauce forms.
  8. Top with chopped parsley.
