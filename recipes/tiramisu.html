Tiramisu

Creamy, rich, and balanced.

Dessert ⏱ ~4+ hrs 🌿 Vegetarian Medium
← All Recipes
dessert

🥛 Cream

  • Egg yolks3
  • Sugar70 g
  • Rum or brandy2 tbsp
  • Mascarpone227 g
  • Heavy whipping cream360 ml

🎂 Cake

  • Lady fingers
  • Espresso1 cup
  • Rum or brandy1 tbsp
  • Cocoa powder

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Combine egg yolks, rum, and 3 tbsp of sugar in a heat proof bowl.

  2. 2

    Set the bowl over a double boiler and keep whisking until the mixture reaches 160 F.

  3. 3

    Cool the egg mixture over a bowl of ice water.

  4. 4

    Add marscapone cheese into the egg mixture.

  5. 5

    Whip the cream until stiff peaks while slowly adding in the rest of the sugar.

  6. 6

    Fold in the whipped cream into the marscapone mixture.

  7. 7

    To make the soaking liquid, add the espresso and rum in a shallow bowl.

  8. 8

    Dip the lady fingers in the liquid and place a layer in the serving tray. Add a layer of the cream mixture and repeat.

  9. 9

    Chill in the fridge for at elast 4 hours.

  10. 10

    Dust with cocoa powder when ready to serve.

💡 Flavor Modification Ideas

  • Strawberry tiramisu.
  • Matcha tiramisu
  • Black sesame and kinako tiramisu.

Ingredients

  • egg yolks (3)
  • sugar (70 g)
  • rum or brandy (2 tbsp)
  • mascarpone (227 g)
  • heavy whipping cream (360 ml)
  • lady fingers
  • espresso (1 cup)
  • rum or brandy (1 tbsp)
  • cocoa powder
