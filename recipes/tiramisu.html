Tiramisu

Creamy, rich, and balanced.

← Back to search

Cream Ingredients

  • egg yolks (3)
  • sugar (70 g)
  • rum or brandy (2 tbsp)
  • mascarpone (227 g)
  • heavy whipping cream (360 ml)

Cake Ingredients

  • lady fingers
  • espresso (1 cup)
  • rum or brandy (1 tbsp)
  • cocoa powder

Instructions

  1. Combine egg yolks, rum, and 3 tbsp of sugar in a heat proof bowl.
  2. Set the bowl over a double boiler and keep whisking until the mixture reaches 160 F.
  3. Cool the egg mixture over a bowl of ice water.
  4. Add marscapone cheese into the egg mixture.
  5. Whip the cream until stiff peaks while slowly adding in the rest of the sugar.
  6. Fold in the whipped cream into the marscapone mixture.
  7. To make the soaking liquid, add the espresso and rum in a shallow bowl.
  8. Dip the lady fingers in the liquid and place a layer in the serving tray. Add a layer of the cream mixture and repeat.
  9. Chill in the fridge for at elast 4 hours.
  10. Dust with cocoa powder when ready to serve.

Flavor Modification Ideas

  • Strawberry tiramisu.
  • Matcha tiramisu
  • Black sesame and kinako tiramisu.
