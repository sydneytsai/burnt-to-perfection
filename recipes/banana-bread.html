Banana Bread

Moist, rich, and perfectly burnt on the edges.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 45g butter
  • 3 bananas
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 70g brown sugar
  • 70g white sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 100g yogurt
  • 60g oil
  • 200g All Purpose Flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Chocolate chunks

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Brown the butter on the stove.
  3. Once the butter is brown, mix in the cinnamon, sugars, bananas, and vanilla.
  4. Cook the bananas and mash while cooking until they smell caramelized and fragrant.
  5. Let the mixture cool for 10 minutes.
  6. Mix together flour and baking soda in separate large mixing bowl
  7. Add yogurt and oil to the cooked banana mixture.
  8. Pour in the wet mixture into the dry mixture and combine thoroughly.
  9. Fold in as many chocolate chunks as you would like.
  10. Pour batter into a loaf pan and bake for 50 minutes, turning the pan 180 degrees halfway through.
  11. Cool for 30 minutes and enjoy!

Storage instructions

  • Banana bread loaf can be left out at room temperature for around 2-4 days.
  • Store in the fridge for up to 7 days.
  • Slice and store in the freezer for up to 3 months.
