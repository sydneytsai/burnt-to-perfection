Chicken Pot Pie

Comforting, buttery, and creamy.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • celery (1 cup)
  • carrots (1 cup)
  • onion or shallots (1 cup)
  • oil (1 tbsp)
  • flour (3 tbsp)
  • butter (3 tbsp)
  • white wine (1/2 cup)
  • chicken stock (2 cups)
  • heavy cream (1/2 cup)
  • parsley
  • chicken (1 cup)
  • pie dough
  • egg (1)
  • salt (to taste)
  • pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Cook and shred chicken.
  2. Heat oil in pan and saute vegetables until just cooked through. Set aside.
  3. Melt butter in pan and slowly stir in flour to make a thick roux.
  4. Add wine and reduce until the alcohol is cooked down.
  5. Preheat oven to 375 F.
  6. Add chicken stock and heavy cream. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Mix in vegetables and chicken.
  8. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  9. Line pan with one round of pie dough and pour in the filling.
  10. Lay on the second round of pie dough and brush with an egg.
  11. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes until dough is fully cooked and a golden brown.
