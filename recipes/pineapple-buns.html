Pineapple Bun

Sweet, pillowy, and buttery.

← Back to search

Milk Bread Ingredients

  • heavy cream (160 ml)
  • milk (235 ml)
  • egg (1)
  • sugar (75 g)
  • cake flour (70 g)
  • bread flour (500 g)
  • instant yeast (11 g)
  • salt (7 g)

Cookie Topping Ingredients

  • butter (50 g)
  • all purpose flour (40 g)
  • egg yolks (2)
  • vanilla (1/2 tsp)
  • baking soda (1/8 tsp)
  • baking powder (1/8 tsp)
  • egg (1)

Instructions

  1. Starting with the bread dough, mix together all ingredients until a dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough for 20 minutes until smooth.
  3. Cover the dough with a damp towel and let is rest in a warm area for 1 hour. The dough should grow 1.5x.
  4. Knead the dough for another 5 minutes to get rid of air bubbles.
  5. Separate the dough into 12 equal rounds and cover with a dry towel to rise for 1 more hour.
  6. To make the cookie topping, add the dry ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly removing any clumps.
  7. Add the wet ingredients and mix together, once it forms a dough like consistency, use your hands to combine into a ball.
  8. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and set aside at room temperature.
  9. Once the buns are done rising, preheat the oven to 350 F.
  10. Separate the topping into 12 equal balls.
  11. Roll out each ball into a flat circle and place on top of the buns.
  12. Score each bun with a bench scraper to make the pineapple shape.
  13. Brush with egg and bake for 12-13 minutes.
