Pineapple Bun

Sweet, pillowy, and buttery.

Dessert ⏱ ~3 hrs 🌿 Vegetarian Medium
← All Recipes
dessert

🍞 Milk Bread

  • Heavy cream160 ml
  • Milk235 ml
  • Egg1
  • Sugar75 g
  • Cake flour70 g
  • Bread flour500 g
  • Instant yeast11 g
  • Salt7 g

✨ Cookie Topping

  • Butter50 g
  • All purpose flour40 g
  • Egg yolks2
  • Vanilla1/2 tsp
  • Baking soda1/8 tsp
  • Baking powder1/8 tsp
  • Egg1

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Starting with the bread dough, mix together all ingredients until a dough forms.

  2. 2

    Knead the dough for 20 minutes until smooth.

  3. 3

    Cover the dough with a damp towel and let is rest in a warm area for 1 hour. The dough should grow 1.5x.

  4. 4

    Knead the dough for another 5 minutes to get rid of air bubbles.

  5. 5

    Separate the dough into 12 equal rounds and cover with a dry towel to rise for 1 more hour.

  6. 6

    To make the cookie topping, add the dry ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly removing any clumps.

  7. 7

    Add the wet ingredients and mix together, once it forms a dough like consistency, use your hands to combine into a ball.

  8. 8

    Cover tightly with plastic wrap and set aside at room temperature.

  9. 9

    Once the buns are done rising, preheat the oven to 350 F.

  10. 10

    Separate the topping into 12 equal balls.

  11. 11

    Roll out each ball into a flat circle and place on top of the buns.

  12. 12

    Score each bun with a bench scraper to make the pineapple shape.

  13. 13

    Brush with egg and bake for 12-13 minutes.

Ingredients

  • heavy cream (160 ml)
  • milk (235 ml)
  • egg (1)
  • sugar (75 g)
  • cake flour (70 g)
  • bread flour (500 g)
  • instant yeast (11 g)
  • salt (7 g)
  • butter (50 g)
  • all purpose flour (40 g)
  • egg yolks (2)
  • vanilla (1/2 tsp)
  • baking soda (1/8 tsp)
  • baking powder (1/8 tsp)
