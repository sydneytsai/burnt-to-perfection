Brown Butter Cornbread Cookies

Crunchy, chewy, and full of texture.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 142g unsalted butter
  • 50g light brown sugar
  • 150g granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking power
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 65g AP Flour
  • 146g cornmeal
  • 1/2 can corn

Instructions

  1. Start by browning the butter in a saucepan over medium heat until brown specks form.
  2. In the slightly cooled butter, mix in the vanilla, sugars, and egg.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350F
  4. In a separate mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients.
  5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and combine.
  6. Fold in the corn kernels.
  7. Bake the cookies for 8 minutes, then tap the sheet to deflate. Continue baking for another 2-3 minutes.
  8. Let the cookies cool and enjoy!
