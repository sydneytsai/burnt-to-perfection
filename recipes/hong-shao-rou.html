Blanching Ingredients

  • 2 green onions
  • Ginger

Ingredients

  • Pork belly
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/3 cup shaoxing wine
  • 2 cups blanching liquid
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • White pepper to taste
  • 1.5 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 2.5 cm ginger
  • 3 green onions
  • 2 star anise pods

Instructions

  1. Cut pork belly into 3/4 inch slices.
  2. Blanch pork belly with ginger and green onions for 1 minute until pork is opaque.
  3. Skim off foam from top of liquid.
  4. Strain the pork belly and reserve some blanching liquid.
  5. Heat the sugar and oil under low heat until brown.
  6. Cook the pork in the sugar until the surfaces are caramelized. The pork does not need to be fully cooked, just caramelized on the outside.
  7. Take out the pork and add cooking wine to the pot. Scrape the pot to get the oil and sugar off the pot.
  8. Add the rest of the braising ingredients and pork belly into the pot.
  9. Simmer the pork until tender and the sauce has reduced. About 1 hour, stirring every 10 minutes.
