Hong Shao Rou

Savory, rich, and fatty.

Main ⏱ ~90 min Medium
← All Recipes
main

💧 Blanching

  • Green onions2
  • Ginger

🧺 Ingredients

  • Pork belly
  • Oil2 tbsp
  • Sugar3 tbsp
  • Shaoxing wine1/3 cup
  • Blanching liquid2 cups
  • Light soy sauce3 tbsp
  • White pepperto taste
  • Dark soy sauce1.5 tbsp
  • Ginger8 g
  • Green onions3
  • Star anise pods2

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Cut pork belly into 3/4 inch slices.

  2. 2

    Blanch pork belly with ginger and green onions for 1 minute until pork is opaque.

  3. 3

    Skim off foam from top of liquid.

  4. 4

    Strain the pork belly and reserve some blanching liquid.

  5. 5

    Heat the sugar and oil under low heat until brown.

  6. 6

    Cook the pork in the sugar until the surfaces are caramelized. The pork does not need to be fully cooked, just caramelized on the outside.

  7. 7

    Take out the pork and add cooking wine to the pot. Scrape the pot to get the oil and sugar off the pot.

  8. 8

    Add the rest of the braising ingredients and pork belly into the pot.

  9. 9

    Simmer the pork until tender and the sauce has reduced. About 1 hour, stirring every 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • green onions (2)
  • ginger
  • pork belly
  • oil (2 tbsp)
  • sugar (3 tbsp)
  • shaoxing wine (1/3 cup)
  • blanching liquid (2 cups)
  • light soy sauce (3 tbsp)
  • white pepper (to taste)
  • dark soy sauce (1.5 tbsp)
  • ginger (8 g)
  • green onions (3)
  • star anise pods (2)
