Beef Enoki Rolls

Comforting dish packed with flavor.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • thin sliced beef
  • enoki mushrooms (1 package)
  • sweet onion (1)
  • eggs (2)
  • dashi (1 cup)
  • green onion tops (handful)
  • soy sauce (3 tbsp)
  • mirin or sugar (2 tbsp)
  • black pepper (1/2 tsp)

Instructions

  1. Roll enoki into beef slices.
  2. Stir fry onion until slightly translucent.
  3. Mix together sauce ingredients in a bowl.
  4. Arrange beef rolls on top of onions.
  5. Drizzle in sauce ad cook for 1 minute.
  6. Drizzle in egg and cook until done.
  7. Garnish with green onion.
  8. Serve with rice and enjoy!
