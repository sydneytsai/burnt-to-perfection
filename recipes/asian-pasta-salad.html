Asian Pasta Salad

Refreshing, flavorful, and healthy.

← Back to search

Tofu Marinade Ingredients

  • firm tofu (1 block)
  • soy sauce (1 tbsp)
  • garlic powder (1 tsp)
  • paprika (1 tsp)

Ingredients

  • pasta (1/2 box)
  • cucumber (1)
  • carrot (1)
  • romaine lettuce
  • soy sauce (1 tbsp)
  • sesame oil (1 tbsp)
  • ponzu (3 tbsp)

Instructions

  1. Cut the tofu into 1/2 inch blocks.
  2. Marinate the tofu in soy sauce, garlic powder, and paprika.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350F
  4. Cook the linguine.
  5. Once cooked, set the pasta aside to cool and toss in sesame oil so the noodles don't stick.
  6. Julienne the carrots and cucumber.
  7. Bake the tofu for 25 minutes until crispy and brown.
  8. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl.
  9. Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
