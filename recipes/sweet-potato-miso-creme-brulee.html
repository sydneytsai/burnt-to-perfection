Sweet Potato Miso Creme Brulee

Creamy balanced dessert.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 5 tbsp roasted japanese yam puree
  • 473g heavy cream
  • 118g sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 5g white miso
  • White sugar for brulee top

Instructions

  1. Boil the cream and vanilla at a medium heat.
  2. Steep for 15 minutes.
  3. Dissolve the miso and yams into the milk. Strain for a better texture.
  4. In a bowl, whisk the sugar and yolks until light in color.
  5. Drizzle in the cream slowly and mix constantly.
  6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes in a water bath.
  7. Sprinkle a thick layer of sugar on each custard and flame torch until crispy and golden.
