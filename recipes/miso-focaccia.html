Miso Focaccia

Savory, umami, and soft.

← Back to search

Focaccia Ingredients

  • 512g All Purpose Flour
  • 15g salt
  • 8g instant yeast
  • 455g lukewarm water
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp olive oil

Miso Butter Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp miso
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 3 cloves garlic

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, salt, and instant yeast in a large bowl.
  2. Add water and stir together dough with spatula until a sticky ball is formed.
  3. Rest the dough for 30 minutes.
  4. Cook the bananas and mash while cooking until they smell caramelized and fragrant.
  5. Strech and fold the dough toward the center 8 to 10 times, turning the bowl 90 degrees each time.
  6. Rub the surface of the dough with olive oil and cover.
  7. Leave in the refrigerator for 24 hours up to 3 days.
  8. Grease a 9x13 inch pan with butter to prevent it from sticking.
  9. Pour the olive oil into the center of the pan.
  10. Deflate the dough by using your fingers to lightly release it from the sides and pulling it to the center.
  11. Place the dough mass into the center of your pan and cover it in the oil.
  12. Let the dough rest for 4 hours at room temperature.
  13. While waiting mix together butter, miso paste, garlic, honey in a small bowl. If the mixture is too thick, microwave until soft in 15 second increments. The resulting texture should be a thick liquid.
  14. Preheat the oven to 425 F
  15. Once the dough has risen, coat your hands in oil and dimple the focaccia byt pressing your fingers straight down into the dough.
  16. Spread the miso butter mixture all over the top of the dough evenly.
  17. Bake the focaccia fror 25 to 30 minutes until the underside is gold and crisp.
  18. Let it cool for 10 minutes and enjoy!
