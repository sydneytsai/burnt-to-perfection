Steamed Buns

Yummy.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • 290g All Purpose Flour
  • 3g instant yeast
  • 3g baking powder
  • 10g sugar
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 180 ml milk

Instructions

  1. Heat milk to 100 F.
  2. Combine all ingerdients and knead for 10 minutes.
  3. Let dough rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Knead dough again until smooth.
  5. Rest the dough for around 1 hour until double in size.
  6. Divide the dough into 10 balls around 48g each.
  7. Form the desired bun shape.
  8. Rest the. dough for 15-30 minutes until 1.5x bigger.
  9. Steam for 10 minutes.
  10. Rest in steamer for 5 minutes without letting any steam out.
