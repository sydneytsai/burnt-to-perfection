Brownies

Chocolatey, fudgy, and chewy.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • brown sugar (200 g)
  • granulated sugar (200 g)
  • unsalted butter (245 mL)
  • room temperature eggs (4)
  • vanilla extract (25 mL)
  • strong coffee (60 mL)
  • cake flour (60 g)
  • all purpose flour (72 g)
  • cocoa powder (93 g)
  • salt (10 g)
  • chocolate chunks (300 g)

Instructions

  1. Brown the butter in a medium heat saucepan.
  2. Preheat the oven to 350F.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sugars and browned butter.
  4. Add in the eggs, vanilla, and coffee. Whish to combine.
  5. In a separate mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients.
  6. Combine the wet and dry ingredients and mix until everything is well combined.
  7. Fold in the chocolate chunks.
  8. Bake for 55-60 minutes. Baking time will vary depending on oven so keep an eye on it.
  9. Cool and enjoy!
