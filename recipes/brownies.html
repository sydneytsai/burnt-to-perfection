Brownies

Chocolatey, fudgy, and chewy.

Dessert ⏱ ~70 min Easy 🌿 Vegetarian
← All Recipes
dessert

🧺 What You'll Need

  • Brown sugar200 g
  • Granulated sugar200 g
  • Unsalted butter245 mL
  • Room temperature eggs4
  • Vanilla extract25 mL
  • Strong coffee60 mL
  • Cake flour60 g
  • All purpose flour72 g
  • Cocoa powder93 g
  • Salt10 g
  • Chocolate chunks300 g

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. 1

    Brown the butter in a medium heat saucepan.

  2. 2

    Preheat the oven to 350F.

  3. 3

    In a large mixing bowl, combine the sugars and browned butter.

  4. 4

    Add in the eggs, vanilla, and coffee. Whish to combine.

  5. 5

    In a separate mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients.

  6. 6

    Combine the wet and dry ingredients and mix until everything is well combined.

  7. 7

    Fold in the chocolate chunks.

  8. 8

    Bake for 55-60 minutes. Baking time will vary depending on oven so keep an eye on it.

  9. 9

    Cool and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • brown sugar (200 g)
  • granulated sugar (200 g)
  • unsalted butter (245 mL)
  • room temperature eggs (4)
  • vanilla extract (25 mL)
  • strong coffee (60 mL)
  • cake flour (60 g)
  • all purpose flour (72 g)
  • cocoa powder (93 g)
  • salt (10 g)
  • chocolate chunks (300 g)
