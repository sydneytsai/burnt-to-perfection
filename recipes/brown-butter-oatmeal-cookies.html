Brown Butter Oatmeal Cookies

Gooey, decadent, and delicious.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • unsalted butter (220 g)
  • brown sugar (200 g)
  • large eggs (2)
  • vanilla (1 tsp)
  • all purpose flour (200 g)
  • oats (175 g)
  • baking soda (1 tsp)
  • salt (1 tsp)
  • chocolate chips (optional)

Instructions

  1. Brown the butter on the stove.
  2. Turn off the heat and stir in vanilla and brown sugar into the butter.
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients.
  4. Once the butter mixture is cooled, mix in the eggs.
  5. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and combine thoroughly.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  7. Chill in the fridge for 1 hour.
  8. Preheat the oven to 350 F.
  9. Roll dough into 15 balls.
  10. Bake for 15 minutes or until golden brown.
