Marry Me Chicken Pasta

Easy, creamy, and delicious.

← Back to search

Ingredients

  • chicken thighs (3)
  • olive oil
  • sundried tomatoes
  • dried oregano
  • paprika
  • garlic
  • pasta
  • heavy cream
  • spinach
  • parmesan
  • salt (to taste)
  • pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. In a pan, heat the olive oil.
  2. Pan fry the chicken until golden brown and crispy on the outside.
  3. Turn the heat to a medium and add the sundried tomatoes, oregano, paprika, and garlic and sautee.
  4. Add the chicken stock and pasta and cook until the pasta is al dente.
  5. Turn the heat low and add the spinach and cream. Cook until the spinach is wilted.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the parmesan.
