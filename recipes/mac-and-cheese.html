Mac and Cheese

Creamy cheesy pasta.

← Back to search

Pasta Ingredients

  • 16 oz pasta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup All Purpose Flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 cups shredded sharp chedder
  • 2 cups gruyere
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • 1/2 tbsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Breadcrumb Topping Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups panko
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F.
  2. Cook pasta until just under al dente and drizzle with olive oil.
  3. Whisk flour into melted butter until bubbly and golden.
  4. Whish in milk and heavy cream until bubbling.
  5. Add salt and pepper to taste. Add in paprika.
  6. Combine the cheddar and gruyere in a bowl.
  7. Add 2 cups of the combined shredded cheese and whisk until smooth.
  8. Add 2 more cups and whisk until creamy.
  9. Stir in pasta.
  10. Pour pasta mixture into an oven safe pan.
  11. Top with last 2 cups cheese and parmesan.
  12. Combine panko with butter and paprika for the crunchy topping.
  13. Layer on breadcrumb topping.
  14. Bake until golden (around 30 minutes).
